# Whole-site redesign brief

## Scope and mode

Every route: the public landing (`/home`, Persuade), auth (`/sign-in`, `/sign-up`), and the role workspaces for donor, NGO, volunteer, tracking and notifications (Operate). A full visual replacement; routes, data, copy meaning, handlers, API calls and behaviour stay identical.

## Audience and job

Both equally: first-time visitors and reviewers must grasp the mechanism in one viewport; donors in busy kitchens, NGO coordinators and volunteer drivers on phones must act fast in daylight. Avoid anything resembling a generic charity look.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A rescue is one continuous kolam line drawn through a dot grid of donor, nearest NGOs and volunteer. It refuses the charity page (soft gradients, smiling stock, heart icons) and the dashboard of identical cards.

OWN-WORLD: Rice-flour white lines and dots on a swept laterite threshold for the landing; the same line in ash ink on limewash for working screens. Kumkum red is the one action colour, turmeric marks what is live, leaf green marks verified. Eczar sets headlines, Mukta everything else, tabular figures for times and distances. Square-shouldered controls, hairline rules, no stacked card shells.

STORY: The visitor sees the line join donor to NGO to volunteer, understands first acceptance wins, and fires a report or joins as a partner. Each role then works on a calm limewash sheet where its rescue's line shows exactly how far the food has travelled.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Dark threshold full-bleed. Left: Eczar headline at about 6rem, one sentence, red "Report surplus food" and outlined "Join the network". Right, two-thirds height: the kolam grid with the live route drawing itself from donor dot to delivered dot, each node labelled. The handoff stages continue below as nodes on the same line.
