Quick reference · domain model

Two state machines

A donation is the overall business object. A rescue assignment is one volunteer attempt to move that donation. They advance at different moments and must remain separate.

Interview anchor

“The donation tells us what is happening to the food; the assignment tells us what is happening to this delivery attempt.”

Donation state

lib/donations/status.ts

REPORTED → MATCHING → NOTIFIED → ACCEPTED → PICKUP_IN_PROGRESS → PICKED_UP → DELIVERED

The assigned NGO may cancel while the donation is active. DELIVERED and CANCELLED are terminal.

Assignment state

lib/rescue/status.ts

OFFERED → ACCEPTED → EN_ROUTE_TO_PICKUP → ARRIVED_AT_PICKUP → PICKED_UP → EN_ROUTE_TO_NGO → ARRIVED_AT_NGO → DELIVERED

An offer can also end as REJECTED, EXPIRED, CANCELLED, or FAILED. These outcomes allow a later attempt.
